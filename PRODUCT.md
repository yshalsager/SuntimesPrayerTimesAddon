# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muslims using Suntimes on Android who need dependable prayer times, prohibited windows, night portions, calendars, alarms, notifications, and widgets across English and Arabic interfaces.

## Product Purpose

Extend Suntimes with accurate, location-aware Islamic prayer information while keeping Suntimes as the source of astronomical calculations. Success means users can trust that every displayed or scheduled time uses the intended location and calculation profile, and failures are explicit rather than plausible but incorrect.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, respectful.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental religious motifs, flashy clock styling, information-dense layouts without hierarchy, decorative effects that compete with times, and ambiguous failure states.

## Design Principles

- Put correctness and location context ahead of decoration.
- Make the next relevant prayer immediately legible.
- Reveal detailed periods progressively instead of crowding primary times.
- Preserve a calm, native Android experience across themes and widget sizes.
- Treat English, Arabic, RTL, and accessibility as first-class behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA-equivalent contrast and interaction clarity. Support English and Arabic RTL, system font scaling, concise complete accessibility labels, color-independent status cues, and reduced-motion system preferences.
